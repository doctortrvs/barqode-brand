# 10 — Website

> This document defines the philosophy behind the Barqode website.
>
> It explains how Barqode should present itself to the world.
>
> It does not define page layouts or implementation details. Those belong in the website itself.

---

# Mission

The purpose of the Barqode website is not simply to explain our software.

Its purpose is to communicate who we are, what we believe and why Barqode exists.

Visitors should leave understanding our ambition long before they understand our features.

The website should feel like the beginning of a movement rather than the homepage of a software company.

---

# First Impression

When someone leaves the Barqode website, they should think:

> "These people understand where logistics is going."

> "They're building for the future, not repairing the past."

> "I'd love to work there."

The website should create belief before it creates demand.

---

# Audience

The website is primarily designed for people who make long-term decisions.

In order of priority:

1. 3PL founders and warehouse owners.
2. Investors.
3. Future employees.
4. Industry partners.
5. Customers evaluating Barqode.

Every page should communicate confidence to these audiences.

---

# Positioning

Barqode should never present itself as another logistics software company.

We are building operational infrastructure for the next generation of fulfilment.

Our competitors improve existing workflows.

We question whether those workflows should exist in the first place.

The future should not look like a faster version of the past.

---

# Personality

If Barqode were a person, they would be quietly unforgettable.

Confident without arrogance.

Stylish without chasing fashion.

Intelligent without needing to prove it.

Precise without becoming robotic.

They appreciate craftsmanship.

They notice details.

They believe simplicity is difficult.

Their confidence comes from competence rather than volume.

---

# Culture

Barqode is built by people who enjoy solving difficult problems together.

We value curiosity over certainty.

Ideas over hierarchy.

Honest discussion over ego.

The best idea wins.

Not the loudest voice.

Not the most senior person.

People should feel comfortable challenging ideas without challenging each other.

We take the work seriously.

We do not take ourselves too seriously.

---

# Story

Barqode exists because we believe logistics deserves better software.

Too many operational platforms are designed around legacy assumptions.

Too many businesses continue repeating the same patterns because "that's how it's always been done."

We believe the next generation of operational software should be designed from first principles.

Not around historical limitations.

But around where fulfilment is going next.

---

# Photography

Photography should feel cinematic rather than corporate.

Warehouses should feel calm.

Organised.

Purposeful.

Almost futuristic.

Images should celebrate operations rather than simply document them.

Automation should feel beautiful.

Infrastructure should feel aspirational.

Real environments should always be preferred over staged marketing photography.

---

# Copywriting

The website should never try too hard to impress.

Confidence comes from clarity.

Short sentences.

Powerful ideas.

Few words.

Every sentence should feel intentional.

Visitors should remember ideas, not slogans.

---

# Trust

Trust should come from competence.

Not exaggerated marketing.

Not inflated claims.

Not unnecessary superlatives.

Demonstrate expertise through thinking.

Show craftsmanship through execution.

Let attention to detail communicate quality.

---

# Recruitment

People should not finish the website thinking:

> "That looks like a nice place to work."

They should finish thinking:

> "I want to help build this."

The website should attract ambitious builders rather than comfortable employees.

---

# Investors

Investors should leave believing Barqode is thinking further ahead than the rest of the market.

Not because we say we are.

Because every detail of the company demonstrates it.

Ambition should feel credible.

Execution should feel inevitable.

---

# Final Principle

The website should not feel like it is selling software.

It should feel like an invitation to join a company that is shaping the future of logistics.

Visitors should leave believing one thing:

> **"These people are building something that matters."**
