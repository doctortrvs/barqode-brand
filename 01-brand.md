# 01 — Brand

> This document defines who Barqode is.
>
> Every design, product, engineering and marketing decision should align with the principles in this document.

---

# Mission

**Barqode exists to connect the world's fulfilment network with the next generation of commerce.**

The barriers to starting a business have never been lower. AI, ecommerce platforms and digital distribution have enabled a new generation of entrepreneurs to build brands faster than ever before. Yet many established warehouses lack the technology, integrations and operating systems required to serve them effectively.

Barqode enables warehouses to transform into modern 3PLs, giving them the infrastructure to onboard, manage and fulfil for multiple ecommerce businesses at scale.

---

# Vision

**A future where any capable warehouse can become a connected, technology-enabled 3PL.**

---

# The Problem

**Commerce has evolved faster than logistics.**

Today, anyone can build a global brand using AI, ecommerce platforms and digital marketing. Yet the fulfilment industry remains fragmented, disconnected and difficult to access. Warehouses, carriers and software platforms operate in silos, while growing businesses struggle to navigate a complex network of providers, integrations and operational processes.

The physical infrastructure already exists. The operational expertise already exists. What many warehouses lack is the technology and operating model required to package that capability as a scalable 3PL service.

**The infrastructure already exists. The connectivity does not.**

---

# Our Belief

**We believe warehouses should not need to rebuild their operations from scratch to become modern 3PLs.**

The expertise, people and physical infrastructure are already there. With the right operating system, established warehouses can connect to ecommerce platforms, carriers and customer systems, onboard new clients quickly and serve the next generation of commerce without years of custom development.

---

# Positioning

**Barqode is the operating system that enables warehouses to become modern 3PLs.**

---

# Audience

## Primary Audience

**Established warehouses with the infrastructure and operational expertise to serve more customers, but without the technology required to operate as a modern 3PL.**

These businesses have already invested in warehouses, people and automation. Their ambition is to diversify beyond serving a single customer or limited customer base, modernise their operations and build a scalable fulfilment business that remains competitive for the next generation of commerce.

---

# Operating Principles

### 1. Make it work.

We solve the real operational problem before polishing the solution.

### 2. Challenge the system.

We question inherited processes before improving them.

### 3. Simplify relentlessly.

Every feature, workflow and decision should reduce operational complexity.

### 4. Build platforms, not projects.

We create systems that solve problems for an entire industry—not bespoke solutions for individual customers.

### 5. Earn trust through visibility.

Operations should never be hidden behind spreadsheets, emails or assumptions. We build transparency into every workflow.

---

# What We Are

* We build platforms, not projects.
* We modernise existing logistics infrastructure.
* We enable warehouses rather than replacing them.
* We remove operational complexity.
* We design for long-term scalability.

---

# What We Are Not

* We are not a bespoke software house.
* We are not a warehouse operator.
* We are not a fulfilment marketplace.
* We do not preserve broken processes because they are familiar.
* We do not add complexity to solve complexity.

---

# Our Promise

**With Barqode, I have complete control over my operation.**

Through speed, simplicity and visibility, Barqode gives warehouses the confidence to grow, modernise and serve more customers without increasing operational complexity.

---

# Success

**The world's fulfilment network is connected, accessible and ready to serve the next generation of commerce.**
