# 05 — Voice

> This document defines how Barqode communicates.

Barqode exists to help people run operations, not software.

Every word, label, notification and message should help users understand what is happening, what needs their attention, and what to do next.

Communication should never slow the operation down.

---

# Communication Mission

Barqode communicates to reduce uncertainty.

Whether someone is connecting their first warehouse, dispatching 10,000 orders or resolving a critical issue, the product should leave them feeling informed, confident and in control.

No matter how complex the problem is, Barqode should communicate one thing above everything else:

> There is an answer.
>
> There is a way forward.

---

# Communication Philosophy

Warehouse operators don't need entertainment.

They don't need buzzwords.

They don't need software trying to sound clever.

They need useful information, delivered quickly.

Barqode assumes the operator is the expert.

It doesn't lecture.

It doesn't patronise.

It works alongside them, providing the right information at the right moment so they can make good decisions with confidence.

The more pressure there is, the calmer Barqode becomes.

---

# Core Principles

## Respect the operator

Operators understand operations.

Our job is to communicate clearly, not simplify their expertise.

Always assume competence.

---

## Every word earns its place

People working in operations don't have time to read paragraphs.

Every sentence should justify its existence.

Remove unnecessary words.

Communicate the same message using fewer words whenever possible.

---

## Clarity before personality

The primary goal of every message is understanding.

If personality reduces clarity, remove it.

---

## Action over explanation

Tell users what happened.

Tell them why.

Tell them what to do next.

Nothing more.

---

## Facts over feelings

Barqode reports reality.

It doesn't exaggerate.

It doesn't dramatise.

It doesn't speculate.

---

## Confidence without ego

Barqode is confident because it is clear.

It never needs to sound impressive.

---

# Writing Principles

Write like operational signage.

Not marketing.

Not literature.

Not conversation.

Prefer:

- Short sentences.
- Familiar words.
- Active voice.
- Specific language.
- Real terminology.

Avoid:

- Long introductions.
- Corporate jargon.
- Marketing language.
- Filler words.
- Repeating the same information.

If a sentence can be made shorter without losing meaning, shorten it.

---

# Product Language

Prefer operational language over software language.

Instead of:

> Configuration successful.

Prefer:

> Warehouse connected.

Instead of:

> Your changes have been saved successfully.

Prefer:

> Changes saved.

Instead of:

> Please proceed to the next step.

Prefer:

> Continue.

The interface should feel like an operating system, not an assistant.

---

# Error Messages

Errors should never create panic.

They should create understanding.

Every error should answer three questions:

1. What happened?
2. Why?
3. What should I do next?

Example:

❌ Something went wrong.

✔ Carrier rejected shipment.

Invalid postcode.

Correct the postcode to continue.

Do not apologise.

Do not use vague language.

Never leave the user wondering what happened.

---

# Empty States

An empty screen is not a dead end.

It is the beginning of a task.

Instead of describing the absence of data, explain the next action.

Example:

No warehouses connected.

Connect your first warehouse to begin.

---

# Success Messages

Success should feel calm.

Avoid exaggerated celebration.

Good work speaks for itself.

Instead of:

🎉 Amazing! You're all set!

Prefer:

Warehouse connected.

Or:

1,250 orders dispatched.

0 exceptions.

Progress motivates more than praise.

---

# Terminology

Use the language operators already use.

Avoid inventing new terms.

If an acronym is widely understood within logistics, use it.

If it is uncommon, explain it once.

After that, trust the user.

---

# Things We Never Say

Barqode never uses language that sounds like marketing.

Avoid phrases such as:

- Revolutionary
- Cutting-edge
- Seamless
- Unlock
- Empower
- Next-generation
- World-class
- Innovative solutions
- Industry-leading

These words add noise without adding meaning.

---

# Voice Attributes

Barqode is:

- Clear.
- Calm.
- Direct.
- Respectful.
- Methodical.
- Confident.

Barqode is never:

- Wordy.
- Patronising.
- Emotional.
- Gimmicky.
- Loud.
- Dramatic.
- Condescending.

---

# Final Principle

Every interaction should leave the operator feeling more confident than they were before.

When people open Barqode during a difficult moment, they shouldn't feel overwhelmed.

They should feel reassured.

Not because the problem is small.

But because the path forward is clear.

Barqode doesn't promise that operations will always go to plan.

It promises that when they don't, you'll always know what to do next.
