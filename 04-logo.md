# 04 — Logo

> This document will define the principles governing the Barqode logo and visual identity.
>
> Unlike the other documents in this repository, the logo has intentionally not been designed yet.
>
> The visual identity should emerge from the philosophy defined throughout this repository rather than the other way around.

---

# Why This Document Is Empty

Most companies begin with a logo and build a brand around it.

Barqode takes the opposite approach.

The brand is being built from first principles:

- Purpose
- Visual language
- Design system
- Voice
- Typography
- Iconography
- Motion

Only once these foundations are complete should the logo be designed.

The logo should be a consequence of the system, not the foundation of it.

# Design Principle

A logo should not explain what Barqode does.

It should represent what Barqode believes.
