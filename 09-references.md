# References

These references inform the Barqode design language.

They are not intended to be copied.

They are studied to understand why they communicate so effectively.

Each reference exists because it teaches a principle.

These references naturally fall into four recurring themes:

- **Recognition** — Communicate before language.
- **Systems** — Design for consistency at scale.
- **Operations** — Support real work in real environments.
- **Calm** — Reduce cognitive load through clarity and restraint.

Together these themes form the visual foundation of the Barqode design language.
